**有效学习：练习、反馈与迁移**关注学习者如何在一段时间后仍能提取知识，并把它用于新问题。策展对应物为 *Astatotilapia burtoni*，仅表示 taxonomy-map 中的分类关联，不对丽鱼行为作任何断言。

## 定义与边界 | Definition and boundaries

有效学习不是单一技巧，而是一组围绕目标、练习、反馈和迁移安排的策略。学习结果至少要说明对象与时间：是在几分钟后认出答案、几周后独立回忆，还是面对不同表述的问题仍能推理。练习时感觉流畅、读过很多遍或能跟着示例操作，都不等于能在没有提示时独立提取。研究常用即时正确率、延迟测验、解释质量或迁移任务作不同指标，指标不能彼此替代。

Dunlosky 等人对十种常见技术的综述把练习测验与间隔练习评为较高实用性，同时指出阐释性提问、自我解释和交错练习的证据适用范围不同，摘要、划线和重读的普遍效用较弱。[C1] Karpicke 与 Blunt 在特定科学文本与概念图条件下发现，检索练习可以比精加工学习带来更好的延迟学习表现。[C3] 这些结果支持认真选择策略，不构成对所有学科、学习者和测验的固定排名。

## 工作机制 | How it works

先把目标拆成可观察能力，再安排从低提示到高难度的练习。检索练习要求先尝试从记忆中回答、画图或推导，再核对资料；反馈指出差距并纠正错误。间隔练习把多次提取分散到不同时间，而不是集中重复。交错练习在容易混淆的问题类型之间切换，训练学习者辨别何时应用哪种方法；但对初学者，过早交错可能增加负担。

练习结果应决定下一步：若只能在看到提示后答对，就缩短提取间隔或补上基础解释；若能稳定完成熟悉题，则改变表面情境检验迁移；若错误集中于概念关系，就用自我解释或反例澄清模型。间隔长度取决于预期保留时间、任务难度和学习者已有知识，不存在对每个人通用的最佳日程。定时回顾错误比累积练习次数更能揭示当前缺口。

## 一个例子 | A worked example

学习者要掌握哈希表冲突处理。第一次学习后合上材料，尝试画出插入、查找和删除过程，并解释负载因子变化；随后对答案，标出链地址法与开放寻址法的混淆点。隔一天再从空白页重建操作，几天后分析一个性能退化案例，说明负载因子、冲突策略和输入分布各自的影响。最后用一个未见过的工作负载决定需要测量什么，而不是照抄课上的结论。这条路线把记忆提取、反馈、间隔和迁移分别变成可观察步骤。

## 局限与误解 | Limits and misconceptions

检索失败本身不会自动教会正确答案；没有反馈，错误回忆可能被保留。把低风险练习变成高压排名会损害参与感，也会促使学习者只优化题目分数。记忆保持的提升不必然迁移到开放式专业任务。研究综述指出效果会随学习条件、材料、先验知识和测量任务变化，许多策略在课堂环境中的证据仍有限。持续练习还需考虑疲劳、可及性、语言背景和动机。应把效果写成具体目标下的可检验假设，并用延迟、近迁移和远迁移的适当测量验证。

**Effective Learning: Practice, Feedback and Transfer** concerns how learners retrieve knowledge after time has passed and apply it to a new problem. Its editorial taxonomic counterpart is *Astatotilapia burtoni*, a catalogue association only; this entry makes no claim about cichlid behaviour.

## 定义与边界 | Definition and boundaries

Effective learning is not one trick. It is a set of choices about goals, practice, feedback, and transfer. An outcome should specify both what was learned and when it is measured: recognizing an answer after a few minutes, recalling it independently weeks later, and reasoning about a differently worded problem are distinct results. A lesson that feels fluent, a passage read several times, or a procedure followed while watching an example does not establish that a learner can retrieve and use the idea without cues. Research therefore uses different measures, including immediate accuracy, delayed tests, explanation quality, and transfer tasks; those measures are not interchangeable.

Dunlosky and colleagues reviewed ten common techniques and rated practice testing and distributed practice highly in general utility. They gave elaborative interrogation, self-explanation, and interleaving more qualified assessments, while summarization, highlighting, and rereading had weaker general support [C1]. The authors explicitly considered differences in learning conditions, student characteristics, materials, and criterion tasks. Karpicke and Blunt found an advantage for retrieval practice over elaborative studying in particular conditions involving science texts and concept mapping [C3]. This supports selecting strategies carefully, not treating the result as a fixed ranking for every subject, learner, and assessment.

## 工作机制 | How it works

Translate the learning goal into an observable capability, then make practice move from stronger cues to more demanding use. Retrieval practice asks the learner to answer, draw, or derive something from memory before checking a source. Feedback reveals a gap and corrects an error. Distributed practice separates retrieval episodes in time instead of concentrating repetition in one session. Interleaved practice switches among problem types that can be confused, helping the learner decide which method applies. For a beginner, however, interleaving too early can add unnecessary load.

Use practice results to choose the next step. If an answer appears only after a hint, shorten the retrieval interval or revisit a missing prerequisite. If a familiar exercise is reliably correct, vary its surface context to probe transfer. If errors cluster around conceptual relationships, use self-explanation or a counterexample to repair the model. The useful spacing interval depends on the desired retention period, task difficulty, and prior knowledge; there is no universally optimal calendar. Reviewing patterns in errors can expose present gaps better than merely counting completed exercises.

## 一个例子 | A worked example

A learner is studying collision handling in a hash table. After an initial lesson, they close the material and draw insertion, lookup, and deletion, explaining how the load factor changes. They then check the result and mark where chaining and open addressing were confused. The next day they reconstruct the operations from a blank page. A few days later they analyze a performance regression and distinguish the possible effects of load factor, collision strategy, and input distribution. Finally, they receive an unfamiliar workload and decide what evidence to measure instead of repeating a conclusion from class. This makes retrieval, feedback, spacing, and transfer separate, observable steps.

## 局限与误解 | Limits and misconceptions

An unsuccessful retrieval attempt does not teach the correct answer automatically; without feedback, a mistaken response may persist. Turning low-stakes practice into high-pressure ranking can reduce participation and encourage learners to optimize only for a test score. Better retention does not guarantee transfer to an open-ended professional task. The major review reports variation across learning conditions, materials, prior knowledge, and outcome measures, and notes that evidence for some methods in classroom settings remains limited. Practice also needs to account for fatigue, accessibility, language background, and motivation. Treat the expected benefit as a testable hypothesis for a specific goal, then use appropriate delayed, near-transfer, and far-transfer measures to evaluate it.
